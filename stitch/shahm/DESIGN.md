## Brand & Style
The design system for "شَهْم" embodies dignity, calm assurance, solidarity, and restorative warmth. Created to facilitate volunteer-driven and compassionate medical transport, the platform balances institutional medical reliability with the heartfelt tenderness of community mutual aid.

The visual style blends **Modern Organic Serenity** with **Tonal Minimalism**:
- **Dignity & Accessibility**: Every screen feels non-stigmatizing, reassuring, and respectful of both patients seeking care and volunteers offering support.
- **Visual Tone**: Warm ivory grounding elements, restorative therapeutic greens, and clear, generous spatial hierarchies reduce anxiety surrounding clinical visits.
- **Tactile Empathy**: Soft micro-surfaces, pillowy rounded modules, and warm natural diffusions instead of sharp, technical, or cold clinic artifacts.

## Colors
The palette is deeply anchored in restorative botanical tones, comforting natural ivory backdrops, and gentle amber touches denoting care, urgency, and hospitality.

- **Primary (`#146B44`)**: Deep Forest Pine. Imparts structural reliability, safety, and deep-seated tranquility. Primary action items, prominent iconography, and active journey markers.
- **Secondary (`#1E8E5A`)**: Healing Jade. Represents active vitality, voluntary contribution, and affirmation. Used for secondary interaction states, confirmation badges, and success highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#D97706`)**: Warm Amber. Applied sparingly for time-sensitive clinic coordination, scheduled pick-up notices, and warm human badges without triggering medical alarm.
- **Neutral (`#24332B`)**: Deep Pine Slate. Provides accessible, high-legibility text contrast while feeling softer and more organic than harsh carbon black.
- **Surface & Foundation**:
  - `surface-canvas`: `#FAF9F5` (Warm Alabaster/Ivory) — reduces eye strain and dispels cold hospital sterility.
  - `surface-subtle`: `#E6F4ED` (Soft Herbarium Mist) — selected for card enclosures, medical detail callouts, and volunteer route containers.
  - `surface-container`: `#FFFFFF` (Pristine Milk) — elevated layers, patient cards, and active route trackers.

## Typography
Typographic rhythm is driven by IBM Plex Sans (with full native Arabic character pairing), engineered for clear legibility across diverse age groups and varying emotional states.

- **RTL Baseline First**: Line heights are expanded compared to Latin baselines to provide breathing room for Arabic ascenders and descenders (kashida, noon, and ya loops).
- **Legibility for Patients & Seniors**: `body-md` is set at a generous 16px with a 26px line height to allow smooth reading without visual clutter.
- **Hierarchical Balance**: High-contrast headline weights (600–700) guide tired eyes effortlessly toward appointment times, destination hospitals, and volunteer contact buttons.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a fluid-responsive grid system designed for effortless one-handed smartphone use during commutes and medical visits, while expanding into an intuitive management hub on desktop devices.

- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column fluid layout with `1rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Critical actions are anchored to accessible thumb zones at the bottom of the screen.
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px)**: 8-column layout with `2rem` margins. Trip maps and appointment logs sit side by side.
- **Desktop (> 1024px)**: 12-column layout maxed out at 1200px width with `3rem` margins to keep medical itineraries readable and comfortable.
- **Pacing Rule**: Use `space-lg` and `space-xl` between logical journey steps to ease cognitive load and establish a relaxed, unhurried cadence.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth conveys security, human closeness, and unpretentious care. Harsh structural drops and harsh dark shadows are replaced with soft ambient cushions tinted with natural botanical green:

- **Level 0 (Canvas Ground)**: `#FAF9F5`. Smooth, matte, and grounding.
- **Level 1 (Subtle Pods / Inactive Containers)**: `#E6F4ED` surface fill with no shadow, bounded by a 1px border of `rgba(20, 107, 68, 0.08)`.
- **Level 2 (Active Cards & Requests)**: Pure white background supported by a warm ambient shadow: `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(20, 107, 68, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(36, 51, 43, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal Sheets & Route Action Banners)**: Elevated panels with deep, comforting pillowed shadows: `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(20, 107, 68, 0.12), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(36, 51, 43, 0.06)`.

## Shapes
The design system implements a **Level 2 (Rounded)** curvature model (`0.5rem` base, `1rem` for primary cards, `1.5rem` for large journey containers and banners).

- **Touchpoints & Inputs**: Inputs and cards employ `1rem` (`rounded-lg`) corner radii to soften edges and create an inviting, safe environment.
- **Buttons & Pills**: Buttons and interaction chips favor full pill rounding (`9999px`) to emphasize human warmth, approachable touch ergonomics, and an uplifting feel.
- **Avatars & Visual Markers**: Volunteer and patient photo avatars use gentle circles with an organic 3px soothing ivory rim.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary ("تقديم يد العون" / "طلب مشوار")**: Full-pill button with background `#146B44`, text `#FFFFFF`, and typography `label-lg`. Padding: `0.875rem 1.75rem`. Soft green glow on hover (`box-shadow: 0 6px 16px rgba(20, 107, 68, 0.25)`).
- **Secondary / Altruistic ("مساعدة مجتمعية")**: Pill button with `#E6F4ED` fill, `#146B44` label, and seamless borderless transition.
- **Text Action**: `#1E8E5A` with subtle underline on focus, prioritizing clear Arabic phrasing over ambiguous icons.

### Journey & Medical Ride Cards
- White surface with `1rem` corner rounding, padded with `1.25rem`.
- Features an integrated status chip at the upper corner (e.g., "بانتظار سائق شهم" in warm amber `#D97706`, "تم تأكيد الموعد" in herbal green `#1E8E5A`).
- Contains clear chronological markers showing pickup home address, clinical destination, and medical appointment department.

### Input Fields & Pickers
- Background filled with `#FFFFFF`, bordered by `1.5px` solid `rgba(20, 107, 68, 0.16)`.
- Corner radius of `0.75rem`. Focused state shifts border to `#1E8E5A` alongside a soft `3px` focus ring tinted in `rgba(30, 142, 90, 0.15)`.
- Helper labels provide practical assistance (e.g., "هل يحتاج الراكب لمقعد متحرك؟").

### Badges & Verification Chips
- Generous internal padding (`0.35rem 0.85rem`) using `rounded-full`.
- Neutral calm style: `#E6F4ED` background with `#146B44` text.
- Trusted Volunteer Badge: Soft herbal gradient with a discreet green checkmark and subtle micro-copy ("متطوع موثوق").

### Checkboxes & Accessibility Radios
- Size: `22px × 22px` for comfortable touch targeting.
- Selected state: `#146B44` fill featuring a crisp white check icon; inactive state displays a `2px` border in `#24332B` at 25% opacity.

### Compassion Banner (شريط الطمأنينة)
- Specialized container at the head of patient scheduling views, using `#E6F4ED` with an encouraging humanitarian message highlighting that transport is complimentary, private, and founded on community brotherhood.
